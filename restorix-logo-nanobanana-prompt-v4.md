Design a clean, modern logo for "Restorix," a B2B AI infrastructure company that serves behavioral health treatment centers.

Icon concept: two simple abstract geometric shapes that interlock or overlap — suggesting connection and continuity (the idea of someone being connected all the way through their care journey, never dropped or lost in the process). Do NOT use literal chain links (too corporate/HR-cliché) and do NOT use a wifi/signal/broadcast wave shape (curved lines radiating from a point — already tried, rejected as too generic/telecom-looking). Do NOT use a letterform (no "R"), animal, mascot, arrow, medical cross, or heart.

Good directions to explore: two offset rounded forms (like two soft circles or rounded squares) overlapping to create a shared negative-space shape in the middle; two simple arcs that cross and lock together; or two abstract rounded shapes nested into each other like a soft yin-yang without the literal yin-yang styling. The overlap/interlock itself should be the visually interesting part of the mark — not decoration on top of it.

Keep it to ONE tight, balanced icon — simple enough to read clearly at a very small size (16x16 favicon scale), not a busy or overly detailed illustration.

Style: flat, vector-style, minimalist tech branding — modern SaaS/health-tech. Should feel calm, precise, and trustworthy, with warmth — this serves people going through a difficult time, not a cold enterprise product.

Layout: generate the icon paired with the wordmark "Restorix" in a clean modern serif or semi-serif typeface, arranged horizontally, icon on the left, text to the right. Also generate a second version with just the icon alone, isolated, for use as a small app/favicon.

Colors: primary color exactly #3a63d6 (a clear medium blue, not navy, not dark) on a white or transparent background. Keep the palette to 1-2 colors max — no gradients, no photorealism, no drop shadows, no 3D rendering.

Typography: the wordmark "Restorix" must render as clean, accurate, sharp lettering — no distorted or garbled letters, all 9 characters legible.

Output: flat vector-style illustration, white/transparent background, high resolution, logo design sheet style (not a photo, not a mockup on a product, not a 3D render).
