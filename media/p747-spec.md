## Prompt 747 — Fulfillment becomes a calendar: a month view with an Activity-style hero, a day drawer that slides in, and the desk as the call view

> **✅ Approved by Brayden 2026-10-10 22:53 CT (Falcon session) after three mockup rounds: "I love the mock-up a lot. This is the best it's looked … good to be queued to CC." Build with Opus 5.5** (a page rebuild over live work data, a month-wide query, the desk folded into a view). Mockup: **`media/fulfillment-calendar/mockup-v3.html`** (the approved one; dark / light and on-a-call toggles bottom-right; v1 / v2 in the same folder are history, ignore them). Match it.

**What changes.** The rep's **Fulfillment** page (`/fulfillment/desk`, sidebar Work → Fulfillment) stops being a one-at-a-time desk with stat tiles and becomes **a month calendar**. Clicking a day **slides a drawer in from the right** with that day's calls. Clicking a call opens **the call view**: today's desk content for that one client, with a back arrow. Brayden's reasons: a rep's work is calls at set times, so a calendar is the easiest page to read, and every agent page has a coloured hero, which this page lacks.

### 1. Page: hero + month grid (`/fulfillment/desk`, no `?open`)

- **Hero** = the **agent Activity page's hero** (`/agent/activity`, P718/P733 `DayHero`) adapted to a month; reuse its component / classes, don't invent a new hero. Centred: a "This month" pill (the current month; other months show the month name only in the pill-less state, your call, keep it quiet), the **month and year** as the big title ("October 2026"), one line of totals with coloured dots: **"57 calls this month · ● 18 cancelled · ● 7 no answer · ● 31 still to call"**, then a white **Open your next call · 3:30 PM** button (to that call's view; hidden when nothing is ahead; "On a call" state keeps the P745 red strip above the hero with **Back to the call**) and a **Go to today** link (jumps to the current month and opens today's drawer). The **round arrows on the hero's left and right edges** change month, exactly like Activity's day arrows (same size, same look; ← / → keys too, ignored while the drawer or call view is open).
- **Month grid** in one `.ov-card` under the hero, full width: **weeks run Sunday to Saturday**; **only that month's days** (blank cells before the 1st and after the last day, never another month's dates); 5 or 6 rows as the month needs. **Weekend tiles look exactly like weekday tiles** (no other colour; carriers are closed then, so they will normally be empty).
- **Each day tile:** the date (today in a filled blue circle + "Today" + a blue ring on the tile), **"N calls"** at the top right, then **up to 3 call chips** (left colour bar + tinted background by status, time in mono, the client's name shortened to fit) and **"+N more"**. Past days dimmed. Hover lifts the tile slightly. A tile is a button ("Tuesday, October 13, 4 calls"); a day with no calls still opens the drawer (empty state).
- **Status colours** (one shared map, see section 5): **Booked, still to call** = booked blue; **On a call** = the live red; **No answer** = the no-answer grey; **Cancelled** = green. A legend sits at the bottom of the card, with "Times are yours (<zone>)" at the right.
- **What a "call" is:** every call the rep has on a day, in the rep's timezone (`profiles.timezone`, the same `lib/fulfillmentDay.js` maths P745 uses): booked calls at their `scheduled_call_at`, No answer retries at their retry time (a client can appear twice on one day, as on P745's Your day), and past calls with how they ended (cancelled / no answer from `policy_events`). **Reuse P745's day logic** (`YourDayCard`'s Up next / Later / Done rules, `useRepCallEvents`) and extend it to a date range; don't write a second set of rules. The month query is one range fetch for the visible month (not one per day), keyed so month changes don't flash.

### 2. The day drawer

- **Reuse My Pipeline's drawer shell** (`ClientDrawer`'s: same width, scrim, 200ms slide-in, P729's slide-out on **X, scrim click and Esc**, then unmount; phones get its full-screen sheet). Don't build a second drawer.
- **Header:** calendar chip, the full date ("Tuesday, October 13") + a "Today" tag when it is, and the **X**. **No previous / next day arrows** (Brayden: only the X, no cycling between days). Under it, one summary line ("7 calls · 3 done · 1 on a call · 3 to go" today; "4 calls booked" future; "4 calls · 3 cancelled · 1 no answer" past) and a thin progress bar split by the status colours.
- **Body:** the day's calls in time order as rows: a time tile (status-tinted), client name, "Carrier · from <agent>" or the useful line (e.g. "Texts sent · retry at 5:30 PM", "4:30 PM for the client (Eastern)" when the client's zone differs), a status pill (Up next / Booked / On a call · N min / No answer / No answer · try 2 / Cancelled), a chevron. On today, a teal **"Now · 2:18 PM"** line sits between past and future rows. Empty day: "Nothing booked on Sat, Oct 10." + "New calls show up here as agents book them."
- **Footer:** primary **Open the next call · <time>** (the first still-to-call row of that day; hidden if none) + "Click any call to work it."
- Clicking a row (or the footer button) **slides the drawer out, then** opens that call's view.
- URL: `?day=2026-10-13` while open (refresh and Back reopen it; cleared after the slide-out). The selected tile is highlighted while its drawer is open.

### 3. The call view (`/fulfillment/desk?open=<id>`)

- This is **today's desk content for one client**, not new work logic: header card (name, status pill, "3:30 PM today · 4:30 PM for the client (Eastern)", "Booked by <agent> · 2 days ago", **Call client** + the number line + **Message agent**), the amber **Cancel with <carrier>** bar (now also the carrier's service phone and "Open until <time> your time" from P728's carrier hours, when known), **Work this call** (the desk's existing steps; **the result buttons and notes are the desk's real ones**, the mock's two buttons are placeholders) beside **Client intake** (same fields as today, missing ones read "Not given"), and a bottom bar **"Next on your calendar · <time> <name> · Next call →"**. Keep every desk action, guard and side effect exactly as it is (claiming, calling, recording a result, messaging); this item only re-homes them.
- **Back arrow** top-left: "← Calendar · Tue, Oct 13" returns to the calendar **with that day's drawer open**. `?open=<id>` keeps working for P745's Overview links ("Open your next call", Up next rows, Back to the call).
- **Removed from the page:** the four stat tiles (Needs attention is gone everywhere on Fulfillment by Brayden's rule; the rest lives on the Overview), "Now working 1 of 20", and the **Desk / Cancelled** switch (cancelled clients are Pipeline's `?stage=cancelled`).

### 4. Decisions Falcon made (Brayden approved the mock without answering these; flag them in the ship note)

1. **One calendar.** This is the Fulfillment calendar; Eagle's earlier idea of a separate Pipeline week calendar is **dropped**. Point P745's **See your calendar** (today `/fulfillment/pipeline?view=calendar`) at `/fulfillment/desk?day=<today>`. Pipeline stays a list.
2. **A booked call whose time passed without a call** shows a quiet amber **"Time passed"** pill in the drawer row (its chip on the tile stays blue). Not a count, not a banner, not "Needs attention".
3. **Admin** (`/fulfillment/desk` as admin): the same page over **every rep's** calls, with an `OvSelect` rep picker in the hero area ("All reps" default, then each rep), and the rep's name on chips' tooltips and drawer rows. Copy swaps "your" for "the team's" where it reads wrong.
4. **Phones:** usable, not polished (mobile is a later roadmap step): under 768px tiles show the date and "N calls" only (no chips), the drawer is the full-screen sheet, the call view stacks.
