Design a clean, modern logo for "Restorix," an AI infrastructure company that serves behavioral health treatment centers (helps clinics capture, qualify, and follow up with patient inquiries so more people get into care).

IMPORTANT — avoid a generic "stock chart growth arrow" icon. That reads as generic finance/SaaS branding and has no connection to healthcare or restoration. Do not use an upward diagonal arrow shape.

Icon concept — pick ONE of these directions and commit to it fully:
(a) An abstract mark built directly from the letter "R" — a geometric monogram where the R's leg is subtly reshaped into a soft curve or gap, suggesting motion/openness, while still clearly reading as "R" at a glance.
(b) A simple, incomplete circular/looping path (like a circle that doesn't quite close, or two arcs forming a soft spiral) — symbolizing a return to wellness/a full-circle journey, without looking like a literal medical or recovery-program cliché (no ribbons, no plus signs, no hearts).

Style: flat, vector-style, minimalist tech branding — modern SaaS/health-tech, not clinical or cold, not playful or cartoonish. Should feel calm, trustworthy, and precise, with warmth — this serves people going through a difficult time, not a finance product.

Layout: a logomark (small icon) paired with the wordmark "Restorix" in a clean modern serif or semi-serif typeface, arranged horizontally, icon on the left, text to the right. Also generate a second version with just the icon alone, sized for use as a small app/favicon.

Colors: primary color exactly #3a63d6 (a clear medium blue, not navy, not dark) on a white or transparent background. Keep the palette to 1-2 colors max — no gradients, no photorealism, no drop shadows.

Typography: the wordmark "Restorix" must render as clean, accurate, sharp lettering — no distorted or garbled letters, all 9 characters legible.

Output: flat vector-style illustration, white/transparent background, high resolution, logo design sheet style (not a photo, not a 3D render, not a mockup on a product).
