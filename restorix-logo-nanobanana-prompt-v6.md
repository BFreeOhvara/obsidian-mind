Design a logomark built from the letter "R" for "Restorix," a B2B AI infrastructure company serving behavioral health treatment centers — but the "R" needs to visually carry the meaning of restoration, not just be a clean generic monogram (a plain R monogram was already tried and rejected as too generic).

Icon concept: an "R" monogram with a deliberate "mend" or "repair seam" running through one stroke — a small visible break or gap in the letterform that is rejoined by a distinct line, notch, or subtle color break, similar in spirit to kintsugi (the art of repairing broken pottery so the repair itself becomes the most beautiful, visible part of the object) — restoration shown through a visible, intentional seam, not hidden or smoothed over. The rest of the R should stay clean and geometric; the mend is the one deliberate design detail that carries the meaning, not decoration layered on top.

Keep it to ONE tight, balanced icon — must still read clearly as the letter "R" at a glance, and must remain legible at a very small size (16x16 favicon scale). Do not make the mend detail so small or fine that it disappears at that scale — it needs to survive being shrunk down.

Style: flat, vector-style, minimalist tech branding — modern SaaS/health-tech. Calm, precise, trustworthy, with warmth.

Layout: the icon paired with the wordmark "Restorix" in a clean modern serif or semi-serif typeface, arranged horizontally, icon on the left, text to the right. Also generate a second version with just the icon alone, isolated, for use as a small app/favicon.

Colors: primary color exactly #3a63d6 (a clear medium blue, not navy, not dark) on a white or transparent background. If using a second accent color for the mend/seam detail, keep it subtle — a lighter tint of the same blue, not a contrasting color. 1-2 colors max — no gradients, no photorealism, no drop shadows, no 3D rendering.

Typography: the wordmark "Restorix" must render as clean, accurate, sharp lettering — no distorted or garbled letters, all 9 characters legible.

Output: flat vector-style illustration, white/transparent background, high resolution, logo design sheet style.
