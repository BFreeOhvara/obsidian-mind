Design a clean, modern logo for "Restorix," a B2B AI infrastructure company that serves behavioral health treatment centers.

Icon concept: an abstract geometric mark — NOT a letterform (do not base it on the letter "R" or any other letter), NOT an animal or mascot, NOT a literal symbol (no arrows, no medical crosses, no hearts, no growth charts). Think of how brands like Stripe, Slack, or Twilio each use a simple, distinctive abstract shape that has no literal meaning tied to the company name, but is clean, geometric, and instantly recognizable on its own. The goal is a mark that becomes ownable and memorable through its shape alone — not because it depicts anything literal.

Build the shape from simple geometric primitives (arcs, circles, angled lines, negative space) combined into one small, tight, balanced form — something that still reads clearly as a single coherent icon at a very small size (16x16 favicon scale), not a busy or overly detailed illustration.

Style: flat, vector-style, minimalist tech branding — modern SaaS/health-tech. Should feel calm, precise, and trustworthy, with a touch of warmth (this serves people going through a difficult time, not a cold enterprise product).

Layout: generate the icon paired with the wordmark "Restorix" in a clean modern serif or semi-serif typeface, arranged horizontally, icon on the left, text to the right. Also generate a second version with just the icon alone, isolated, for use as a small app/favicon.

Colors: primary color exactly #3a63d6 (a clear medium blue, not navy, not dark) on a white or transparent background. Keep the palette to 1-2 colors max — no gradients, no photorealism, no drop shadows, no 3D rendering.

Typography: the wordmark "Restorix" must render as clean, accurate, sharp lettering — no distorted or garbled letters, all 9 characters legible.

Output: flat vector-style illustration, white/transparent background, high resolution, logo design sheet style (not a photo, not a mockup on a product, not a 3D render).
